# U1-Adapted and P0 initial engineering audit

U1-Adapted is newly implemented, not a historical validated U1 and not an official checkpoint. The LiDAR-only input duplicates a binary input-observation mask into two channels after 2× max pooling to F1, preserving the official conditional branch's duplicated-mask input idea. Geometry and masking use existing FP32/FP64 routines. The P0 down-only branch is an adaptation of the official `lidar_cond_input_emb` and `lidar_cond_downs`: it has three 3D GN/SiLU residual stages and F3 spatial attention, plus three 128D output adapters. It is not claimed to be a bitwise copy of the original patch-size network.

F1/F2/F3 internal down widths are 32/64/128. U1 adds F4 width 256, two 256-channel middle residual blocks, and nearest-neighbor convolution upsampling followed by skip concatenation and residual blocks, producing F3/F2/F1 widths 128/64/32. Each final scale is independently projected to 128. F3 uses single-head full spatial SDPA. No A/B/C modules are implemented in these baselines. All three pyramid outputs match the requested ZYX shapes.

P0 and U1 passed real maximum-scene batch-2 forward/backward, finite gradients and checkpoint/EMA roundtrip under FP32 weights + FP16 autocast. Peak allocated was 4,697,946,112 and 5,374,742,528 bytes respectively, including Stage1 Flow and backward; see `u1_adapted_audit/gate_*.json`. Initial no-grad profiling hit a Q/K/V stride error; contiguous layout fixed it, and training and no-grad GPU checks were repeated. Two CPU tests, including same-seed shared initialization and attention arithmetic, passed.

Batch-1 single-frame warmed RTX3090 CUDA-event results (3 warmups, 5 timing iterations; no formal multi-frame benchmark): P0 encoder 13.22–13.35 ms, 1,132,032 parameters; U1 encoder 30.56–33.07 ms, 16,056,288 parameters. Counted convolution plus attention matrix multiplication: P0 80.78 GMAC, U1 324.02 GMAC. These counts omit indexing, normalization, activations, interpolation and memory operations, and do not establish comparison to the original strong Encoder. Full records: `u1_adapted_audit/profile_*.json`.

Each 32-update smoke starts scratch with Batch2, seed 20261032 and the matched independently seeded FM Flow. The smoke is separate from future formal weights. P0/U1 2K, 5K, 20K and all 41-frame quality: NOT TESTED. A/B/C/controls: NOT IMPLEMENTED, NOT TESTED. Plan after the U1 baseline is validated: GPU2 U1+A, U1+A+B, B control; GPU3 U1+A+C, C control, U1+A+B+C, with 5K for the full combination regardless of small single-arm deltas. This is a plan, not an active queue.

P0 and U1 have each completed 32 successful smoke updates (32 attempts, zero AMP skips) with the same Flow initialization SHA256 `6efa2c64da812878dd70b883153bd6e04d897a864c19637f71b5b79a37875ddd`. `run_patch_first5k.py` starts a separate fresh model after checking smoke/source identity, trains to 2K, evaluates a fixed non-seq08 dev41, resumes to 5K with optimizer/EMA/scaler/scheduler/RNG, evaluates the same dev41 and stops for review. It does not queue or imply completed A/B/C work.
